Week 04

Make It Remember

The project needs to remember something between visits — and you need to survive real feedback without deflecting it.

Architecture

Decide how state survives

Where does data live between sessions, and what happens the moment it's unavailable? Document both.

  1. Pick the persistence approach — file, local DB, cloud store — and state why in one sentence.
  2. Write the failure mode: what does the app do if that store is empty, slow, or gone?
  3. Add this as ADR-002.
persistence state graceful degradation failure mode
Build the project

Add persistence, ship feature #2

Wire in the state/persistence decision from the architecture rep, and ship a second feature that depends on it — something that wouldn't make sense without memory.

  1. Implement the store and confirm data survives a restart.
  2. Build feature #2 on top of it.
  3. Deliberately empty the store once and confirm the app fails the way ADR-002 says it should.
Claude fluency

Context management

Conversation memory, context-window budgeting, and when to summarize versus replay full history.

Teach me context management for my app
My app now needs to remember [describe what feature #2 remembers] across a session. Explain the tradeoffs between replaying full conversation history, summarizing older turns, and storing structured state outside the conversation entirely. Recommend which fits my case and why, then show a minimal code pattern for it.
Stress-test my persistence design
Here's ADR-002 on how my app persists state: [paste it]. What's the most likely way this quietly corrupts or loses data in production, and what's the one-line mitigation you'd insist on before shipping?
Presentation & inhibition

Demo to two people, ask for real critique

Rung 4 of 10

Same demo format as Week 3, now to two people together. Explicitly ask: "What's the one thing you'd change?" When they answer, say only "thank you, noted" — no explaining, no defending. Write the feedback down after, unfiltered.

Done when
  • State persists across a restart and fails the way you designed it to.
  • Feature #2 is shipped and depends on real persisted data.
  • You can explain summarization vs full-history vs external state in your own words.
  • You received critique from two people without defending yourself in the moment.
