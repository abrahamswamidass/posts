Design Under Pressure

Design cold, under a clock, then check your work. Ship the integration that makes this feel like a real, live thing.

Architecture

Timed scaling exercise, no Claude

Set a 20-minute timer. Paper or whiteboard only. Question: "How would you scale this to 100x users?" Write your answer before the timer runs out. Only then, check it against Claude.

capacity planning back-of-envelope estimation bottleneck
Check my scaling answer
I designed this scaling approach cold, in 20 minutes, for [describe your app]: [paste your answer]. Where's the biggest hole in it, and what would you have asked me that I didn't address?
Build the project

An integration that makes it feel real

Connect the project to something live — a real scheduled job, a real external data source, a real deploy target — whatever makes it stop feeling like a demo and start feeling like a thing that runs without you.

Claude fluency

LLM-as-judge

Have Claude score its own past output against a rubric you write, rather than trusting your own read of "looks fine."

Build me an LLM-as-judge rubric
Here's what a good answer from feature #1 looks like: [give 2-3 examples]. Help me write a scoring rubric (1-5, with criteria) I could use to have Claude grade a batch of its own past outputs, and show me the prompt that would do the grading.
Presentation & inhibition

Send a recording to one person, unedited

Rung 8 of 10

Record a walkthrough and actually send it — unedited, no re-takes — to one person. Ask for their honest reaction, and don't preface it with "sorry, it's rough."

Done when
  • You have a written, timed scaling answer and Claude's critique of it side by side.
  • One real integration is live — a scheduled job, external source, or deploy target.
  • You have a written rubric and at least one batch of self-graded outputs.
  • You've sent an unedited recording to someone and gotten a real reaction back.
