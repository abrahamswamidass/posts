Where you land

Twelve weeks ago this was a paragraph and a napkin sketch. Now it's a deployed thing you built with Claude as an actual collaborator, an architecture doc you wrote and can defend under questioning, and ten presentation reps behind you on a curve that went from an empty room to a live audience. Whatever you build next quarter, start it the same way: pick something uncomfortable, keep all four threads running every week, and don't let a bad week hide for a month before you notice it.
