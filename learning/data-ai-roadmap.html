Why this plan, for you

You are not starting from zero. You already ship data engineering at Cardinal Health — Palantir Foundry pipelines and ontologies, Airflow and Dataflow, BigQuery, Kafka microservices, SQL tuning at scale, plus twenty years of enterprise architecture and a healthcare/PII lens most engineers never develop. This roadmap does not re-teach that. It does three things:

  • Puts the vocabulary at your fingertips — you already do dimensional modeling, idempotency, and medallion architecture. Months 1–6 give you the precise industry terms so you lead the whiteboard instead of following it.
  • Modernizes the stack — dbt, Spark, lakehouse table formats, streaming, DataOps. The tools the market screens for in 2026.
  • Builds the AI half from the ground up — ML foundations, how LLMs actually work, RAG, agents, MCP, and LLMOps — ending with a production AI system that fuses both halves of your skill set.
How to use it — the LLM learning loop

You said you may not get to read books. Good news: you don't have to. Every month page ends with copy-paste Claude prompts engineered to replace a textbook — they ask for diagrams, analogies, worked examples, quizzes, and mock interviews. Reading is replaced by conversation.

  1. Open the month. Skim "What you'll master" and the terminology at the bottom so you know the shape of the territory.
  2. Learn by prompting. Paste each prompt into Claude. Ask follow-ups until the concept clicks. Request a Mermaid or ASCII diagram whenever words aren't enough — Claude renders architecture beautifully.
  3. Do the hands-on challenge. This is non-negotiable — the vocabulary only sticks when you've built the thing. Use Claude as a pair programmer and reviewer.
  4. Prove it. Tick off the "Done when" checklist. If you can teach the month back to Claude and survive its mock interview, you own it.

Budget ~6–8 focused hours a week. The plan assumes free tiers throughout: BigQuery sandbox, Databricks Community Edition, Snowflake trial, a local Postgres, and the Anthropic API.

Phase 1 · Months 1–6

Data Engineering & Architecture

Own the vocabulary, modernize the stack, ship a lakehouse platform.
MONTH 01
Foundations & the Language of Data Architecture
OLTP vs OLAP, ELT, dimensional modeling, star schemas, SCDs, the medallion architecture — the words you'll use in every design meeting.
MONTH 02
The Modern Data Stack & Cloud Warehousing
Snowflake / BigQuery / Databricks internals, separation of storage & compute, and dbt as the transformation layer everyone expects you to know.
MONTH 03
Distributed Batch Processing with Spark
Spark's execution model, shuffles, skew, joins, and the lakehouse table formats — Delta, Iceberg, Hudi — that put ACID on the data lake.
MONTH 04
Streaming & Real-Time Data
Kafka in depth, stream processing, windows and watermarks, exactly-once semantics, CDC, and the Lambda vs Kappa debate.
MONTH 05
Orchestration, DataOps & Data Quality
Airflow and asset-based orchestration, data contracts, quality gates, lineage, observability, and CI/CD for data pipelines.
MONTH 06
Architecture, Governance & Capstone I
Data mesh, governance, FinOps, platform system design — then build and document an end-to-end lakehouse. Portfolio piece #1.
Phase 2 · Months 7–12

AI & LLM Engineering

From ML foundations to a production, evaluated, monitored AI system.
MONTH 07
Machine Learning Engineering Foundations
The ML lifecycle, features, evaluation metrics, the train/serve split, and a first production model — the ground floor of AI work.
MONTH 08
Neural Nets, Embeddings & How LLMs Work
Attention, transformers, tokenization, embeddings, and the path from next-token predictor to Claude — demystified, with visuals.
MONTH 09
LLM Application Engineering & RAG
The Claude API, prompt engineering, structured output, and retrieval-augmented generation end-to-end with a vector database.
MONTH 10
Agents, Tool Use & the Model Context Protocol
Function calling, the ReAct loop, MCP, guardrails, and a data-analyst agent that queries your own warehouse in plain English.
MONTH 11
Production AI Systems & LLMOps & Capstone II
Evals, LLM-as-judge, tracing, cost/latency, fine-tune vs RAG — then ship an AI system fused with your data pipeline. Portfolio piece #2.
MONTH 12
System Design, Portfolio & Job Launch
Data & AI system-design interviews, STAR stories from your career, resume repositioning, mock loops, and the application sprint.
Where you land in 12 months

By month 12 you will hold two documented capstone systems, a rebuilt resume that reads "data + AI engineer," and — more importantly — the ability to sit in an architecture review and reach for the right term without hunting for it. Grain. Idempotency. Watermark. Exactly-once. Embedding. Retrieval. Eval. Guardrail. You'll know what each one means, when to use it, and why the alternative is worse. That fluency, plus a portfolio that proves it, is what converts twenty years of experience into a new title.
